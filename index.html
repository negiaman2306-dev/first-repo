Instagram dashboard 2026

The Instagram Dashboard 2026 built in power BI to analyzes 16M+ views and 2M+ likes across creators. It tracks engagement rating, video duration, virality score and niche performance. Key finding. The dashboard exceeded its virality goal by 64% and helps identify top creators and hashtags for content strategy

Car Sales Dashboard

This car sales dashboard shows total price of 1.48B, 1,184 units sold, and 4,121 customer rating from 1,000 records. toyota leads with the highest sales value, followed by mahindra and honda. It analyzes annual income vs age, payment methods(cash, credit/debit, loan, UPI), annual income vs engine size, company vs price, and price vs body style, helping to understand customer demographics, buying preferences and top-performing brands.

Product Sales Dashboard

This Tableau Sales dashboard tracks 2.24M sales amount, 322K cost and 2.274 quantity across Acessories ans Electronics. It shows year-wise trends, product-wise cost, category distribution, city-wise and customer-wise quantity to analyze sales performance and buying patterns.

Global EV Adoption Dashboard 2026

The Global Ev adorption 2026 dashboard presents key metrics for EV adoption, including annual income, daily commute distance, electricity cost per kWh, battery lifespan cost, environmental awarness, and monthly fuel expenses. It includes charts for city type distribution, weekly travel distance, monthly energy consumption, and battery replacement vs government incentive awarness. With filters for current vehicle type, previous Ev experience and age, the dashboard helps understand how economic, behavorial and environmental factors influence the shift to electric vehicles.
